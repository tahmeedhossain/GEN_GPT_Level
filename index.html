Nonclassicality Network

A curated, signed, typed theorem graph of quantum foundations, information, thermodynamics and resource theories — built so that the absence of an edge means "unknown", impossibility theorems are first-class signed edges (⊣), and the uncharted cells enumerate open problems. Two pages, one network; below, the record of propositions the structure itself produced.

Why it exists — three uses: automated research-idea generation (the gap census is the agenda: every gray cell is a candidate theorem with four closure routes); easy reviewing (one claim = one cited, statused edge, so checking the review means auditing edges and disputing it means naming an edge id); and visualization (a field's logical shape — flows, blocks, blind spots — at a glance). The review process is itself the generator: surveying the field this way yields its own research agenda.

How the network generates theorems

1 · SIGNED EDGES

Possibility theorems (→) and impossibility theorems (⊣) are both recorded; an absent edge therefore means nobody has settled it, never "false."

2 · ENUMERATE GAPS

The witness-algebra matrix and the typed layers list every unsettled cell and node pair mechanically.

3 · CLOSE OR REGISTER

Each gap is closed by composing recorded edges, by a direct proof, by a counterexample, or by a literature fill — otherwise it is registered as a named open problem.

4 · RE-RUN

New edges change the gap census; the loop repeats. Three rounds are recorded below.

Still uncharted (examples)

  • Athermality × contextuality — is there a free-energy budget for contextuality under thermal operations? No theorem either way.
  • Hidden-contextuality activation (SG5) — do local filters amplify the sequential-scenario contextuality of Bell-local entangled states as they do hidden nonlocality?
  • Covariant dynamics × magic — generation or monotone decay? The two resource theories have never been crossed.
  • Sorkin order of the toy field theory (SG4) — I₃ of a non-quantum-subtheory epistricted model is uncomputed.
