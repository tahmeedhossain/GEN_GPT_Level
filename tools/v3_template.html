Nonclassicality Network III: hypergraph form

The layered theorem network v2.6 with hyperedges. A statement that needs several nodes at once is one edge that joins all of them: an entangled state and a process that can see entanglement fire the witness; a free state and a free operation cannot create the resource. An ordinary edge is the special case of one premise and one conclusion. Dots are hyperedges: one spoke per premise, one arrow (possibility) or flat bar (no-go) per conclusion. Reading the drawing. A curve between two boxes is a binary edge of v2.6: arrowhead for possibility, flat bar for no-go. A dot is a hyperedge: every line that reaches the dot without a terminator is a premise, every line that leaves it with an arrowhead or a flat bar is a conclusion. A filled dot is a possibility statement, a hollow dot a no-go. Colour is provenance as before (teal theorem, copper derived here, red counterexample, grey definition); dashed spokes mark a statement about some states only, dotted spokes a requirement. Click a dot, a spoke or a box. The three signatures that are off by default (requirements, unary records, complements) are the former witness records turned into edges.
